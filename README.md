Booked & Busy Now — Marketing Platform

Live site: bookedandbusynow.co

Marketing website for Booked & Busy Now, a US-based digital agency delivering local SEO, conversion-focused web design, and paid advertising for service businesses. The site is the agency's primary sales and lead generation tool, positioning it against generic "marketing partners" with a direct, results-focused value proposition.

Project Scope
Conversion-focused homepage with bold hero, problem-framing sections, and strong CTAs
Services breakdown covering local SEO, technical SEO, analytics, and online review management
Client testimonials and social proof sections
Stats section with business metrics (600+ wins, qualified leads, clicks tracked)
Lead capture integration with calendar booking flow
Responsive mobile-first layout
Key Design Decisions

The site is built around the agency's positioning: direct, honest, and focused on outcomes rather than design aesthetics. Copy and structure are engineered to convert service business owners who are frustrated with marketing spend that doesn't produce leads.

Results

Live site serving the agency's client acquisition pipeline across the United States.
